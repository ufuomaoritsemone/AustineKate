THE VISION

Sophistication Meets Functionality

AUSTINKATE COURTS is a premier 4-block, 4 and 5-storey luxury residential apartment complex featuring 28 tastefully-finished private residences in Benin City.

Conceptualized by Zehava Concept Limited and Trafex Limited, this project introduces a novel approach to unparalleled luxury. Enriched with premium-quality materials, elegant modern architecture, and expert facility management, it represents the ideal location to live and play.

Each residence is meticulously designed to support a contemporary lifestyle, blending structural elegance with smart spaces and sustainable infrastructure.
