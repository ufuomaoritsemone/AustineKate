JEFFERSON UWOGHIREN, ESQ 

Jefferson Uwoghiren, Esq., is a distinguished legal practitioner whose life journey reflects resilience, integrity, intellectual growth, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. His professional path began in journalism, where he cultivated strong analytical abilities, effective communication skills, and a deep respect for truth and public enlightenment. His experience as a journalist laid a solid foundation for his future endeavours, instilling in him a keen sense of responsibility and a passion for societal impact.

Jefferson Uwoghiren is the Managing Director of Trafex Limited, a perception management company incorporated in 1992. Under his leadership, the company has successfully executed high-profile projects for several government organizations and reputable private companies, earning a reputation for excellence, integrity, diligence, and professionalism. Throughout his career, he has remained committed to upholding the rule of law, advocating for justice, and providing exceptional legal services to his clients.



CHARLES AKHIGBE 

Charles Akhigbe is business executive, management and strategy consultant with over three decades of multifaceted experience spanning financial services, agribusiness, real estate advisory, sales and marketing.

In addition to a robust risk management, business development and deal execution capacity, he has over the years profitably led multiple business and turnaround teams in Allstates Trust Bank, FSB International Bank Plc, Fidelity Bank Plc and Wondar Mills Limited.

Charles Akhigbe was Head, Public Sector, Midwest Region at Fidelity Bank Plc prior to leading the Zehava Concepts Nigeria Limited team.
